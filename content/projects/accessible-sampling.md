## Research problem

This project studies how to select line-chart samples that support efficient trend analysis for blind users.

## My contribution

I developed an optimization pipeline that applies a machine-teaching framework to accessible sampling.

## Methods

The approach uses perceptual optimization to select samples for chart analysis.

## Research output

The work forms the manuscript *Machine teaching for accessible line chart sampling*, which is in preparation.
