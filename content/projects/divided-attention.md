## Research problem

People often interpret visualizations while performing other tasks. This project examines how divided attention affects perception, mapping, and comprehension.

## My contribution

I designed controlled experiments and modeled users' cognitive processes with the Linear Ballistic Accumulator framework.

## Methods

The work combines experiments on visualization interpretation with a model of the decision process.

## Results

The research identified increased reliance on expectation-aligned visualization designs under divided attention.
