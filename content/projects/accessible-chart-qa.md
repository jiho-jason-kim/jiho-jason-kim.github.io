## Research problem

Question-answering systems can offer another way to explore chart content. This project examines the needs of blind and low vision users.

## My contribution

I analyzed blind users' interactions with chart question-answering systems.

## Results

The work identified design requirements for a more accessible chart question-answering system.

The findings were published at ACM CHI 2023.
