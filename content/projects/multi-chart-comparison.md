## Research problem

Chart comparison depends on more than spatial arrangement. This work examines how layout, visual marks, and comparison tasks interact.

## My contribution

I characterized layout through the joint effects of layout, mark, and task. I used this framework to predict viewer performance across charts.

## Methods

The framework connects visualization design choices with comparison performance.

## Research output

The work forms the manuscript *Comparison Axis Alignment: Characterizing the Interaction of Layout, Mark, and Task in Multi-Chart Comparison*.
