## Research problem

The name of a chart type does not explain its structure to someone unfamiliar with it. This work explores accessible text explanations.

## My contribution

I designed strategies for explaining unfamiliar chart types, including treemaps, using learning theories and automatic text generation.

## Methods

The project investigates different ways to communicate chart structure through text.

## Research output

The work was published at ACM CHI 2023. Gyeongri Kim and I contributed equally.
